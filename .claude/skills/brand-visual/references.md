## Staxen — getstaxen.com
- Very similar to Gaudi; use as a direct competitive benchmark.
- Like: step-by-step flow, ease-of-integration emphasis, easy onboarding, "no credit card needed," clean visuals.
- Dislike: personified tasks (images + names of "people" like Eddie/Josie/Ivy). Keep task names as job titles instead — more intuitive for users.
